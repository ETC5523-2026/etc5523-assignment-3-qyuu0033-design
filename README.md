# ETC5523 Blog Assessment

## About the Blog

**Title:** Fire Weather & Climate Risk

This blog explores how climate change influences extreme fire-weather risk in southeastern Australia, using the 2019–20 Australian bushfire season as a case study. It communicates climate attribution research in an accessible way and discusses the implications for climate adaptation and emergency preparedness.

## Author

Qingqing Yu

Master of Business Analytics (B6022), Monash University.

## Intended Audience

This blog is written for local government professionals and emergency management practitioners who need to understand changing climate risks and their implications for long-term planning and bushfire preparedness. It aims to communicate scientific evidence clearly without assuming specialist knowledge of climate attribution research.
